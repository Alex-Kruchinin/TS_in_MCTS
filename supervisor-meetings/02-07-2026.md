# Supervisor Meeting — 13 July 2026

## Topics discussed

Showed the game state structure, including board representation, legal move generation, terminal-state detection, and reward calculation.

Presented the match-running structure used to play games between two agents, and explained how the `RandomAgent` is used as the initial baseline opponent.



Presented the current MCTS implementation structure, including:

* `MCTSNode` for representing search-tree nodes;
* parent and child links in the search tree;
* visit counts and accumulated rewards;
* expansion of untried moves;
* rollout / simulation from newly expanded nodes;
* backpropagation of rollout results through the selected path.
* Explained the UCT selection policy and how the formula balances exploitation and exploration.



Discussed how MCTS is used during a match:

* the agent receives the current board state;
* it performs a fixed number of internal simulations;
* it selects one real move;
* after the opponent responds, a new search is performed from the updated board state.
